A collection of the assignments, registers, reports and audit documents I produced during my **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) internship (July - August 2026)**, centred on implementing an Information Security Management System (ISMS) in line with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**.

> **Author:** Muneeb Ahmed | Bachelor's in Cyber-Security
> **Program:** GRC Internship 2026 | **Host:** NCERT

---

## About This Repository

The internship followed the ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer path, from understanding why an ISMS matters to building the documents an auditor expects to see. This repo holds that work in one place:

- Risk management artefacts (asset register, risk assessment, risk register)
- ISMS governance documents (PISF report, ISMS business case)
- Compliance documents (Statement of Applicability, internal audit checklist and report)
- Applied scenario work (Annex A control mapping, E-Scooter project)

## Internship Roadmap

| Week | Focus | Key outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Orientation, GRC fundamentals, ISMS basics, mandatory clauses, PDCA | Pre-assessment, Lead Implementer notes |
| **2** | SoA and gap analysis, ISMS importance, risk management foundations | ISMS report, PISF report, asset register |
| **3** | Risk assessment and register, ISO 27001 roadmap, Clause 4, Annex A mapping | Risk assessment, risk register, Week 3 assignments |
| **4** | Policy documentation, internal audit, PDCA (Plan and Do) | SoA, audit checklist, internal audit document |
| **5** | Incident response plan, KPIs, final review | Assignment 05, project |

## Skills and Concepts Demonstrated

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** Clauses 4-10, Annex A controls, SoA, PDCA cycle
- **Risk management:** asset identification, risk assessment, risk register, risk treatment
- **Governance and policy:** ISMS business case, security policy documentation
- **Audit and compliance:** internal audit planning, audit checklists, gap analysis
- **Incident management:** incident response planning and KPI definition
- **Professional practice:** structured documentation, case-study analysis, tools including Excel, Word and PowerPoint

## How to Use

Download or clone the repository and open the files directly. No installation is needed.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/muneebahmed-siddiqui/GRC-ISO27001.git
```

- `.pdf` files preview directly on GitHub.
- `.docx` and `.xlsx` files should be downloaded and opened in Microsoft Office or LibreOffice.
